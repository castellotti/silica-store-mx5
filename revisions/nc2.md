# MX-5 NC2 (2009 facelift), model years 2009–2012

## Introduction

Presented at the 2008 Paris Motor Show and later at the 2009 Chicago Auto Show; the Japanese model went on sale on December 9, 2008. [^wp-nc]

## Changes

2009 facelift: restyled front with a larger grille and new head and fog lights; restyled side skirts, rear bumper and tail lights; darker instrument panel with redesigned gauges; door pocket protrusion removed for more leg space. [^wp-nc]

## Engine

2.0 L; 122.0 cu in (1,999 cc) I4: 125 kW (167 bhp) at 7,000 rpm and 190 N⋅m (140 lb⋅ft) at 5,000 rpm with the 5-speed manual; 118 kW (158 bhp) at 6,700 rpm with the automatic. Manual redline raised by 500 rpm to 7,200 rpm. [^wp-nc]

## Trims

Soft-top Touring and Grand Touring models had a mesh grille bordered by a chrome frame; the hardtop PRHT Roadster Coupe added chrome elements inside the headlamps. [^wp-nc]

## Special editions

Roadster 20th Anniversary (Japan, 2009); 20th Anniversary Model (Europe, 2010, 2,000 produced); MX-5 Miyako (UK, 2010); 2011 Special Edition (US, 750 units); MX-5 Sport Black (UK, 2011); MX-5 Miata Special Edition (US, 2012, 450 units). [^wp-nc]
