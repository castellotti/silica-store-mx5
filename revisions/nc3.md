# MX-5 NC3 (2012 update), model years 2013–2015

## Introduction

Introduced in July 2012 for the 2013 model year. [^wp-nc]

## Changes

Coordinated black and silver features, a larger grille opening, revised fog lamps, a new lip spoiler, a luxury tan interior option, Active Bonnet pedestrian protection, a lighter front bumper and new 17-inch aluminum wheels. The brake booster was modified to better modulate braking. [^wp-nc]

## Engine

For the 2013 model year no modifications were made to the powerplant; the ECU of manual cars was retuned for a more linear throttle response. [^wp-nc]

## Trims

US: the Club trim replaced Touring, adding black headlamps and mirror covers, gun metal black alloy wheels, Bilstein dampers, a limited-slip differential and red stitched black cloth seats; Club colors were limited to True Red, Brilliant Black, Crystal White Pearl and Liquid Silver Metallic. The Grand Touring could add the Club's suspension parts as the Suspension Package. [^wp-nc]

## 25th Anniversary Model

1099 units of the PRHT with the 2.0L engine, all in Soul Red Metallic, with matched pistons, a lightened flywheel and a forged crankshaft; on sale in the UK on August 1, 2014. [^wp-nc]
