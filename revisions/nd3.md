# MX-5 ND3 (2024 update), model years 2024–present

## Introduction and changes

In February 2024: new infotainment, new headlights with daytime running lamps, taillights with reverse lights, a new type of limited-slip differential, a revised steering rack, a minor facelift and a 'dynamic stability control - track' mode. [^wp-nd]

## Compliance action (Japan)

On 3 June 2024, Japanese-market Roadster RF deliveries were suspended over rewritten engine control software found during power output tests; deliveries resumed on 28 June 2024. [^wp-nd]

## Engine

Specification table, 2.0 L (2019–): 135 kW (181 hp; 184 PS) and 205 N⋅m (151 lbf⋅ft). [^wp-nd]

## Curb weight

The infobox base figure is for the 1.5 L manual (S/Sport), not the engine of American and Canadian models: ND2/ND3 add 8 kg (18 lb) to the ND1 base of 990 kg (2,183 lb); automatic adds 20 kg (44 lb); RF adds 50 kg (110 lb). [^wp-nd]
