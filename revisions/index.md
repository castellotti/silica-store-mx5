Major and minor revisions within each generation; see the [glossary](../model/glossary.md) for the codes.

* [MX-5 NC3 (2012 update), model years 2013–2015](nc3.md) - NC3 revision of the NC MX-5: 2012 update: Club trim, styling and the 25th Anniversary.
* [MX-5 ND1, model years 2016–2018](nd1.md) - ND1 revision of the ND MX-5: launch, engines, RF and weights.
* [MX-5 ND2 (2018 update), model years 2019–2023](nd2.md) - ND2 revision of the ND MX-5: 2019 engine update, trims, fuel economy and the 2022 change.
