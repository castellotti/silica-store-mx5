# 2005 Mazda MX-5

## Generation and revision

Second generation (NB), after the 2001 facelift; updated models are known as NB2 and earlier ones as NB1. [^wp-mx5]

## Trims

Includes the turbocharged Mazdaspeed MX-5 (Roadster Turbo in Japan), introduced for the 2004 model year. [^wp-nb]

## Colors

_Not found._

## Special editions

Mazdaspeed MX-5: Lava Orange Mica and Black Mica added for 2005 in the US market; the 2005 production run was shortened to 1,428 due to a fire at the production facility. [^wp-nb]

## Engine

BP6D 1.8 L, with variable valve timing on the intake camshaft: 107 kW (143 bhp) in the US; 113 kW (152 bhp) in Japan, the UK and Australia. [^wp-nb]

## Engine (Mazdaspeed)

Mazdaspeed MX-5: IHI RHF5 turbocharged BP-4W producing 133 kW (178 bhp) at 6,000 rpm and 226 N⋅m (167 lbf⋅ft) at 4,500 rpm, with a front-mounted air-to-air intercooler. [^wp-nb]
