# 1999 Mazda MX-5

## Generation and revision

Second generation (NB), before the 2001 facelift. The MX-5 article says the NB was sold beginning in 1998 for the 1999 model year. [^wp-mx5]

## Trims

_Not found._

## Colors

_Not found._

## Special editions

10th Anniversary Model: 7,500 units in Sapphire Blue Mica (Innocent Blue in Japan) with a 6-speed manual, Bilstein shocks and a Torsen limited-slip differential; 3,000 went to the US. [^wp-nb]

## Engine

BP-4W 1.8 L: compression raised from 9.0:1 to 9.5:1 and Mazda's Variable Intake Control System added; 104.4 kW (140.0 bhp) and 157 N⋅m (116 lbf⋅ft). The 1.6 L (98 cu in) B6 engine remained available in Europe and Japan. [^wp-nb]

## Curb weight

_Not found._

## Fuel economy

10th Anniversary Model (6-speed): combined 8.0 L/100 km (35 mpg‑imp; 29 mpg‑US), improved from 8.5 L/100 km for the standard 5-speed model. [^wp-nb]

## Awards

Car and Driver 10Best list (1998-1999). [^wp-mx5]

## Features

Fixed headlamps replaced the retractable units; glass rear window with defogger; optional hinged wind deflector; anti-lock braking system optional; 14-inch or 15-inch alloy wheels depending on trim; Cd=0.36. [^wp-nb]
