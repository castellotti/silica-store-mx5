# 2016 Mazda MX-5

## Generation and revision

Fourth generation (ND), first phase (ND1). Production began on 4 March 2015; model years 2016–present (North America). [^wp-nd]

_Not found._

## Special editions

UK: MX-5 Icon, 600 cars in Meteor Grey Mica or Crystal White Pearlescent. MX-5 Levanto: a one-off with Garage Italia Customs. [^wp-nd]

## Engine

Skyactiv-G direct-injection petrol engines: a 1.5 L of 96 kW (129 hp; 131 PS) in base models; pre-2019 American and Canadian models use the 1,998 cc 2.0 L rated at 116 kW (155 hp; 157 PS) at 6000 rpm and 201 N⋅m (148 lb⋅ft) at 4600 rpm. [^wp-nd]

## Curb weight

ND1 1.5 L manual base (S/Sport): 990 kg (2,183 lb); automatic adds 20 kg (44 lb); RF adds 50 kg (110 lb). [^wp-nd]

## Awards

Car of the Year Japan Award 2015–2016; 2016 World Car of the Year and World Car Design of the Year; Car and Driver 10Best list (2016 to 2019); Automobile magazine "All-Stars" 2016; 2016 UK Car of the Year; The Daily Telegraph 2016 Car of the Year; Wheels 2016 Car of the Year; Yahoo! Autos 2016 Fresh Ride of the Year; Roadshow by CNET Editors Choice Best Convertibles 2016. [^wp-nd]

## Features

105 mm shorter and 100 kg lighter than the NC; Electric Power Assisted Steering replaced hydraulic power steering; six-speed manual or six-speed automatic; manually operated fabric roof. [^wp-nd]
