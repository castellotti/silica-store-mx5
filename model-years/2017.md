# 2017 Mazda MX-5

## Generation and revision

Fourth generation (ND), first phase (ND1). Production began on 4 March 2015; model years 2016–present (North America). [^wp-nd]

## Trims

_Not found._

## Colors

## Special editions

UK: Launch Edition (RF), 500 models at £28,995 with the 2.0-litre engine, in Soul Red or Machine Grey. [^wp-nd]

## Engine

Skyactiv-G direct-injection petrol engines: a 1.5 L of 96 kW (129 hp; 131 PS) in base models; pre-2019 American and Canadian models use the 1,998 cc 2.0 L rated at 116 kW (155 hp; 157 PS) at 6000 rpm and 201 N⋅m (148 lb⋅ft) at 4600 rpm. [^wp-nd]

## Curb weight

ND1 1.5 L manual base (S/Sport): 990 kg (2,183 lb); automatic adds 20 kg (44 lb); RF adds 50 kg (110 lb). [^wp-nd]

## Awards

Car and Driver 10Best list (2016 to 2019); Auto Express 2017 Roadster of the Year; Red Dot Best of the Best Award: Product Design 2017. [^wp-nd]

## Features

Retractable Fastback (RF) model with a rigid roof; the RF has a full-colour circular digital gauge and introduced the 'Machine Grey Mica' paint colour. [^wp-nd]
