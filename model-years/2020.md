# 2020 Mazda MX-5

## Generation and revision

Fourth generation (ND), 2018 update (ND2), introduced in late 2018 for the 2019 model year. [^wp-nd]

## Trims

The GT-S features (limited-slip differential, Bilstein shock absorbers, strut tower brace) became standard on the GT in 2020 and the GT-S designation was retired. [^wp-nd]

## Colors

_Not found._

## Special editions

100th Anniversary in Snowflake White Pearl Mica with Garnet Red Napa leather; France: Eunos Edition, 110 units in Jet Black Mica. [^wp-nd]

## Engine

Specification table: 2.0 L (2019–) 135 kW (181 hp; 184 PS) and 205 N⋅m (151 lbf⋅ft); 1.5 L (2015–) 96 kW (129 hp; 131 PS) and 150 N⋅m (111 lbf⋅ft). [^wp-nd]

## Curb weight

ND2/ND3 add 8 kg (18 lb) to the ND1 1.5 L manual base of 990 kg (2,183 lb); automatic adds 20 kg (44 lb); RF adds 50 kg (110 lb). [^wp-nd]
