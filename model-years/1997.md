# 1997 Mazda MX-5

## Generation and revision

First generation (NA), 1.8 L period, from the freshening for the 1994 model year; the NA was phased out after the 1997 model year. [^wp-na]

## Trims

US: the base model with the A Package, B Package and C Package continued; the R Package, introduced in 1994, added Bilstein shocks, stiffer sway bars, retuned springs, underbody spoilers and a Torsen LSD, and was not available with power steering, leather or an automatic transmission. Annual special editions were formalized as M Editions. [^wp-na]

## Special editions

Final M-Edition in Marina Green Mica (3,000 units, USA); Speedster Edition for Canada (150 units); Special Touring Option (STO) in Twilight Blue Mica (1,500 units); Monza Edition for the UK (800 made). [^wp-na]

## Engine

1.8 L BP-ZE inline four-cylinder, raised for the 1996 model year to 99 kW (133 bhp) at 6,500 rpm and 155 N⋅m (114 lbf⋅ft) at 5,500 rpm. [^wp-na]

## Curb weight

Base weight 990 kg (2,180 lb) after the 1994 changes. [^wp-na]
