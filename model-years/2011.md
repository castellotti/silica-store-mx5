# 2011 Mazda MX-5

## Generation and revision

Third generation (NC), 2009 facelift (NC2), presented at the 2008 Paris Motor Show and the 2009 Chicago Auto Show. [^wp-nc]

## Trims

Soft-top Touring and Grand Touring models had a mesh grille bordered by a chrome frame; the hardtop PRHT Roadster Coupe added chrome elements inside the headlamps. [^wp-nc]

## Colors

_Not found._

## Special editions

US: 2011 Special Edition, 750 PRHT units in Dolphin Gray Mica or Sparkling Black Mica. UK: MX-5 Sport Black, 500 examples. Japan: Roadster BLACK TUNED. [^wp-nc]

## Engine

2.0 L; 122.0 cu in (1,999 cc) I4: 125 kW (167 bhp) at 7,000 rpm and 190 N⋅m (140 lb⋅ft) at 5,000 rpm with the 5-speed manual; 118 kW (158 bhp) at 6,700 rpm with the automatic. Manual redline raised by 500 rpm to 7,200 rpm. [^wp-nc]

## Curb weight

Generation figures (infobox): 1,110 kg (2,450 lb) soft-top; 1,153 kg (2,542 lb) PRHT. [^wp-nc]
