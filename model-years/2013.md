# 2013 Mazda MX-5

## Generation and revision

Third generation (NC), 2012 update (NC3), introduced in July 2012 for the 2013 model year. [^wp-nc]

## Trims

US: the Club trim replaced Touring, adding black headlamps and mirror covers, gun metal black alloy wheels, Bilstein dampers, a limited-slip differential and red stitched black cloth seats; Club colors were limited to True Red, Brilliant Black, Crystal White Pearl and Liquid Silver Metallic. The Grand Touring could add the Club's suspension parts as the Suspension Package. [^wp-nc]

## Special editions

UK: BBR MX-5 GT270, 100 cars at 200 kW (268 bhp); Jota MX-5 GT, 151 kW (203 bhp). [^wp-nc]

## Engine

For the 2013 model year no modifications were made to the powerplant; the ECU of manual cars was retuned for a more linear throttle response. [^wp-nc]

## Curb weight

Generation figures (infobox): 1,110 kg (2,450 lb) soft-top; 1,153 kg (2,542 lb) PRHT. [^wp-nc]

## Features

Coordinated black and silver features, a larger grille opening, revised fog lamps, a new lip spoiler, a luxury tan interior option, Active Bonnet pedestrian protection, a lighter front bumper and new 17-inch aluminum wheels. [^wp-nc]
