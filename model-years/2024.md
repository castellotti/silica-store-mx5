# 2024 Mazda MX-5

## Generation and revision

Fourth generation (ND), 2024 update (ND3), introduced in February 2024. [^wp-nd]

## Trims

_Not found._

## Colors

_Not found._

## Special editions

Poland: Flat Out Edition, developed with Marcin "Radzio" Radecki, 2 litre only. [^wp-nd]

## Engine

Specification table: 2.0 L (2019–) 135 kW (181 hp; 184 PS) and 205 N⋅m (151 lbf⋅ft); 1.5 L (2015–) 96 kW (129 hp; 131 PS) and 150 N⋅m (111 lbf⋅ft). [^wp-nd]

## Curb weight

ND2/ND3 add 8 kg (18 lb) to the ND1 1.5 L manual base of 990 kg (2,183 lb); automatic adds 20 kg (44 lb); RF adds 50 kg (110 lb). [^wp-nd]

## Awards

Car and Driver 2024 Editor's Choice: Affordable Sports Car. [^wp-nd]

## Features

New infotainment, new headlights with daytime running lamps, taillights with reverse lights, a new type of limited-slip differential, a revised steering rack, a minor facelift and a 'dynamic stability control - track' mode. [^wp-nd]

## Compliance action (Japan)

On 3 June 2024, Japanese-market Roadster RF deliveries were suspended over rewritten engine control software found during power output tests; deliveries resumed on 28 June 2024. [^wp-nd]
