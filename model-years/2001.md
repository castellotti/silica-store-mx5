# 2001 Mazda MX-5

## Generation and revision

Second generation (NB), after the 2001 facelift; updated models are known as NB2 and earlier ones as NB1. [^wp-mx5]

## Trims

Top models (designated 'Sport' in the U.K) added 16-inch wheels, larger brakes, chassis braces, a limited-slip differential, a 6-speed manual, Bilstein suspension and leather seats. [^wp-nb]

## Engine

BP6D 1.8 L, with variable valve timing on the intake camshaft: 107 kW (143 bhp) in the US; 113 kW (152 bhp) in Japan, the UK and Australia. [^wp-nb]

## Curb weight

1,065 kg (2,348 lb) with the minimum of options (2001 model). [^wp-nb]

## Awards

Car and Driver 10Best list (2001). [^wp-mx5]

## Features

2001 facelift: fog lamps made standard, projector low beams with separate high beams, clear rear turn signals, a redesigned center console, white faced and red backlit gauges and more supportive seats; top models added 16-inch wheels with low-profile tires, larger brakes, a limited-slip differential, a 6-speed manual and Bilstein suspension. [^wp-nb]

## Power rating buy-back

Mazda's early US catalogues quoted the Japanese and Australian power figure; Mazda offered to buy back the 2001 cars, and owners who kept them were offered an apology and free servicing for the warranty period. [^wp-nb]
