# 2019 Mazda MX-5

## Generation and revision

Fourth generation (ND), 2018 update (ND2), introduced in late 2018 for the 2019 model year. [^wp-nd]

## Trims

US: Sport, Club and Grand Touring (Canada: GS, GS-P and GT). A GT-S trim brought the Club's limited-slip differential, Bilstein shock absorbers and strut tower brace to the GT, with a black roof on hard-top models. [^wp-nd]

## Colors

_Not found._

## Special editions

30th Anniversary Edition in Racing Orange: 3,000 worldwide, 500 allocated to the U.S. plus an additional 143. [^wp-nd]

## Engine

2.0 L revised to 135 kW (181 hp; 184 PS) and 205 N⋅m (151 lb⋅ft), redline 7,500 rpm, with a dual-mass flywheel; in North America the ratings are at 7,000 rpm and 4000 rpm. [^wp-nd]

## Curb weight

ND2/ND3 add 8 kg (18 lb) to the ND1 1.5 L manual base of 990 kg (2,183 lb); automatic adds 20 kg (44 lb); RF adds 50 kg (110 lb). [^wp-nd]

## Fuel economy

Claimed (North America, 2.0 L): manual 9.0 L/100 km (26.1 mpg‑US) city and 7.0 L/100 km (33.6 mpg‑US) highway; automatic 9.0 L/100 km (26.1 mpg‑US) city and 6.6 L/100 km (35.6 mpg‑US) highway. [^wp-nd]

## Recalls

_Not found._

## Awards

Car and Driver 10Best list (2016 to 2019); Edmunds.com 2019 Editor's Choice Awards: Best Sports Car. [^wp-nd]

## Features

Telescoping steering column, standard reverse camera, reworked door opening mechanism, revised seat controls and redesigned cupholders; burgundy soft top and black 17-inch wheels; dual-mass flywheel. [^wp-nd]
