# 2003 Mazda MX-5

## Generation and revision

Second generation (NB), after the 2001 facelift; updated models are known as NB2 and earlier ones as NB1. [^wp-mx5]

## Trims

US: the Shinsen Version (SV) sat between the base model and the pricier LS. [^wp-nb]

## Colors

_Not found._

## Special editions

Shinsen Version (SV) in titanium silver with a dark blue top and interior; Miata Club Sport (MCS), 50 units for the SCCA (25 Classic Red, 25 Pure White); Roadster Coupé for Japan only, 179 units. [^wp-nb]

## Engine

BP6D 1.8 L, with variable valve timing on the intake camshaft: 107 kW (143 bhp) in the US; 113 kW (152 bhp) in Japan, the UK and Australia. [^wp-nb]
