# Mazda MX-5 Cup

## Overview

The Whelen Mazda MX-5 Cup presented by Michelin is a single-make championship sanctioned by the International Motor Sports Association (IMSA); its roots are in 2003 and a unified national championship began in 2006. [^wp-cup]

## 2003–2005 cars

The Mazdaspeed Miata Cup accepted first and second generation cars built from 1990 through 1993 with a 1.600cc engine at a minimum weight of 2.300lbs, from 1994 through 1998 at 2.350lbs, and from 1999 through 2005 at 2.400lbs. [^wp-cup]

## 2006–2015 cars

Third generation MX-5 cars with the standard 2.0L engine prepared by Mazdaspeed and a stock 6-speed manual; model years 2006 through 2014 were accepted. [^wp-cup]

## 2016–present cars

Fourth generation MX-5 cars with the 2.0 L Skyactiv-G (PE-VPS); ND1 from 2016 to 2018 and an ND2 class from 2019 with a Bosch ECU and a SADEV sequential gearbox. [^wp-cup]
