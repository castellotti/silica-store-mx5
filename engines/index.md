# Engines

Engine families used in the MX-5.

* [Mazda B6ZE(RS) 1.6 L](b6ze-rs.md) - The 1.6 L B6ZE(RS) inline-four developed for the MX-5 (NA, and NB in Europe and Japan).
* [Mazda BP 1.8 L (BP-ZE, BP-4W, BP-Z3)](bp.md) - The 1.8 L BP inline-four family used in the NA from 1994, the NB and the Mazdaspeed MX-5.
* [Mazda MZR L-series (L8 1.8 L, LF 2.0 L)](mzr-l.md) - The MZR L-series four-cylinder engines used in the NC MX-5.
* [Mazda Skyactiv-G 1.5 L and 2.0 L in the MX-5](skyactiv-g.md) - The Skyactiv-G P5-VPS 1.5 L and PE-VPS 2.0 L engines of the ND MX-5.
