# Glossary of MX-5 codes

## Generation codes

NA, NB, NC and ND are Mazda's generation codes, one per generation (see [Mazda MX-5](/model/mx-5.md)).

## Revision codes

This store names revisions within a generation NA1, NA2, NB1, NB2, NC1, NC2, NC3, ND1, ND2 and ND3. NB1/NB2, NC1–NC3 and ND1–ND3 appear in the cited Wikipedia articles. The cited NA article does not number the NA's revisions. This store uses NA1 for the 1.6 L cars (model years 1990–1993) and NA2 for the 1.8 L cars (1994–1997).

## Model years

Model years are North American model years unless a fact says otherwise. A magazine list year (such as a 10Best year) is recorded as published, which is not always the model year.

## Body styles

PRHT: Power Retractable Hard Top (NC). RF: Retractable Fastback (ND).
